Trade mark knowledge foundationProof of concept

Australian trade mark law and examination guidance, turned into rules and code that people and programs can check.

The Trade Marks Act 1995 and the Trade Marks Regulations 1995 set out the law. IP Australia’s Trade Marks Manual of Practice and Procedure explains how the Registrar examines applications. It describes practice; it is not law. This site keeps the two apart.
