About the project

  • Results and limits

The Trade Marks Act 1995 and Trade Marks Regulations 1995 set out the law. IP Australia’s Trade Marks Manual of Practice and Procedure explains how the Registrar examines applications. It describes practice; it is not law. This site keeps the two apart.

Labels show where AI wrote the content. Nothing here decides an application.
