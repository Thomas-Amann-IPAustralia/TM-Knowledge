Trade marks guidePrototype

A prototype, for review. It has not been tested with customers, and it is not an official service.

  • Answers are written by AI from the Trade Marks Act 1995, the Trade Marks Regulations 1995 and IP Australia’s Trade Marks Manual. No trade marks expert has checked them.
  • The Act and the Regulations are the law. The Manual is IP Australia’s guidance on how examiners apply it; it is not law. Each answer keeps the two apart.
  • It is general information, not legal advice, and it never says how an application will be decided. Only the Registrar decides.
  • Tap “Where this comes from” under any answer to see the exact words it rests on.
  • Please don’t type personal details, application numbers or the text of a real report.
